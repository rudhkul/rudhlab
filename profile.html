ABOUT

Engineering questions, explored computationally.

I am Anirudh Kulkarni, an Assistant Professor at PDEU in Gandhinagar, India. My research includes computational modelling, dental simulation, fluid dynamics, and GPU computing.

RudhLab brings these interests together as a growing research group, with a focus on numerical methods and scientific software.

Research leadership

I participate in three ongoing research projects: one as Principal Investigator and two as Co-Principal Investigator.

The group includes one Junior Research Fellow and one part-time PhD student.
