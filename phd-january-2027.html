About the group

RudhLab is in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Pandit Deendayal Energy University (PDEU), Gandhinagar. Our work brings together fluid mechanics, numerical analysis, and high-performance computing. We develop our own simulation software and use it to study fluid flow, heat transfer, and coupled physical phenomena.

We are looking for students who want to understand not only what a simulation predicts, but also why its numerical method can be trusted.

Research directions

GPU-accelerated CFD and scientific software

Efficient, accurate solvers in CUDA C++ using lattice-Boltzmann, finite-volume, and immersed-boundary methods, with attention to conservation, stability, verification, and performance.

Multiphase and interfacial flow

Droplet and bubble motion, interface evolution, phase-change processes, and multiphysics coupling.

Flow instability, turbulence, and control

Vortex shedding, bluff-body wakes, transition, and the effects of porous structures and complex geometries.

Computational heat transfer and sustainable energy

Natural convection, thermal management, passive cooling, and coupled thermal–fluid problems.

Projects may also use physics-guided machine learning, reduced-order modelling, or uncertainty quantification where these offer a defensible scientific benefit. See our research and simulations.

What the PhD involves

  • Mathematical modelling of the flow or heat-transfer problem.
  • Implementing the method, in the group’s in-house software and open scientific-computing tools.
  • Verification and validation against analytical solutions and reference data.
  • Physical interpretation of the results.
  • Writing up the work for publication.

Want a head start? Work through the getting started guide.

Who should enquire

Applicants from mechanical, aerospace, chemical, and related engineering fields, and applicants with an applied mathematics or computational science background, may be considered where eligible under PDEU’s admission rules.

Sound fundamentals and an interest in developing computational tools are essential. Experience with Python, C/C++, Julia, OpenFOAM, Linux, or GPUs helps, but prior CUDA experience is not required.
