METHODS & APPLICATIONS

What we work on

Alongside the funded projects, we work on numerical methods and their applications in fluid and solid mechanics.

01

Fluid dynamics
& heat transfer

Flow around bluff bodies, porous-media transport, vortex-induced vibration, and multiphase flow. We develop finite-volume, lattice-Boltzmann, and immersed-boundary solvers.

Watch the simulations ↗
02

Dental
mechanics

Finite-element studies of tooth movement and orthodontic appliances, including molar protraction loops and implant-supported systems.

Read the publications ↗
03

GPU solvers
& research code

CUDA C++ and Julia implementations of numerical methods. Current development includes interface capturing, pressure solvers, and GPU-accelerated fluid–structure interaction.
