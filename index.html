Numerical solvers for fluid flow, heat transfer, and dental mechanics.

RudhLab is a computational research group at Pandit Deendayal Energy University, Gandhinagar, led by Anirudh Kulkarni. We write our own solvers, from lattice-Boltzmann to immersed-boundary methods, and run them on GPUs.

Research

Fluid dynamics and heat transfer

Flow around bluff bodies, porous-media transport, vortex-induced vibration, and multiphase flow, studied with finite-volume, lattice-Boltzmann, and immersed-boundary solvers.

Simulations →

Dental mechanics

Finite-element studies of tooth movement and orthodontic appliances, including molar protraction loops and implant-supported systems.

Publications →

GPU solvers and research code

CUDA C++ and Julia implementations of numerical methods: interface capturing, pressure solvers, and GPU-accelerated fluid–structure interaction.
