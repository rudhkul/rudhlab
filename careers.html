PhD research

Enquiries welcome

Admission is through the PDEU PhD programme. If your interests match the group’s research, write before applying so we can discuss a topic. Formal calls and application details will be added here.

What you would work on

Stepwells and passive cooling

How air and heat move through Gujarat’s historic stepwells, using photogrammetry and lattice-Boltzmann simulation. Funded by the Ministry of Education (IKS Division).

Fluids for underwater exploration

Lattice-Boltzmann simulation and machine learning to characterise complex fluids. Funded by the IIT Guwahati Technology Innovation Hub.

GPU solvers

Fast, conservative solvers for multiphase and phase-change flow, written in CUDA C++ and Julia.

Dental and computational mechanics

Finite-element models of teeth and orthodontic appliances, in collaboration with clinicians.

These are examples of current directions. All funded projects · Simulations · Publications

What you will learn

  • Writing simulation software from the equations up, not only running commercial packages.
  • Numerical methods: finite-volume, lattice-Boltzmann, immersed-boundary, and finite-element.
  • GPU programming in CUDA C++ and Julia, and working on Linux with Git.
  • Open-source tools such as OpenFOAM and ParaView.
  • Checking results against reference data, and writing them up for publication.

Who we are looking for

There is no single required background. Students from mechanical, chemical, aerospace, or civil engineering, physics, or applied mathematics have the right foundation. Some programming experience (Python, C/C++, Julia, or MATLAB) helps, and so does curiosity about why a flow or structure behaves the way it does. Formal eligibility for each position is stated in its call.

How to apply

Email Anirudh with “JRF” or “PhD” in the subject line, and include:

  1. Your degree, institution, and year of graduation.
  2. One or two topics from this site that interest you, and why.
  3. Anything you have built: code (a GitHub link is ideal), a project report, or a thesis.
  4. Your CV as a PDF.

Email Anirudh →anirudh.kulkarni@sot.pdpu.ac.in
